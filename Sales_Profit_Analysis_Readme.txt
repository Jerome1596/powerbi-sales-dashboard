# Sales Dashboard (with Profit Analysis)

This Power BI dashboard provides interactive visualizations and insights into sales performance, profit trends, and product category breakdown.

## 🚀 Features
- **Total Sales Card** – Highlights total revenue in an eye-catching format.
- **Time Series Chart** – Monthly trend of total sales and profit to monitor business performance over time.
- **Regional Map View** – Visualizes sales distribution across geographic regions.
- **Category-wise Sales & Profit Donuts** – Breaks down metrics by product categories for deeper analysis.
- **Top Product Profit Bar Chart** – Identifies high and low-performing products by total profit.

## 📊 Tools & Techniques
- Power BI Desktop  
- DAX for calculated measures  
- Power Query for data transformation  
- Visuals: Card, Line Chart, Map, Donut Chart, Column Chart

## 📁 Dataset
Sample Superstore Sales Data
